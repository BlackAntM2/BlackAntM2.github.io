# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: players arriving from the @ant_tech_ Instagram "link in bio" on their phone, who want to see the games and tap through to the App Store. Secondary: App Store review and anyone checking the studio is real, who need the privacy policies and a support contact.

## Product Purpose

The home of ANTTECH, a one-person indie studio making small iPhone games. It exists to send players to each game's App Store page and to host the legal pages the App Store requires. Success is a visitor reaching a game's App Store page within one tap of landing.

## Positioning

Small, finished, free iPhone games made by one developer. No publisher, no accounts, offline play.

## Operating Context

Hosted on GitHub Pages at blackantm2.github.io (static HTML/CSS, no build step, pushed from the developer's Mac). Visitors are mostly on iPhones, arriving cold from Instagram Reels.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Games shown: Tap Against Time (live, App Store id6755980854), Dockside: Cast Off (in App Review, id6827859226; its link works once Apple approves), Circuit Weave (in development, no download link yet). Star Blaster is deliberately not shown.
- These URLs must keep working because App Store listings and AdMob point at them: `/privacy.html` (Tap Against Time policy), `/dockside-privacy.html`, `/site-privacy.html`, `/app-ads.txt`, `/games.html` (Instagram bio link), `/promo/**` (videos used by scheduled Instagram posts).
- Contact: anttech1081@gmail.com.

## Brand Commitments

- Studio name on the site: ANTTECH. The legal developer name, ANT TECH SOLUTIONS, appears only in the privacy policies.
- Logo: `assets/anttech-logo.png`.
- Each game keeps its own look; the site does not restyle the games.

## Evidence on Hand

- App icons and store screenshots: Dockside in `~/Harbour Jam/Store/screenshots/final/iphone/`; Tap Against Time and Circuit Weave screenshots can be captured from the simulator.
- Gameplay reels in `/promo/dockside/` and `/promo/tap-against-time/`.
- Absent, never to be invented: reviews, ratings, download counts, press quotes, player numbers.

## Product Principles

1. One tap to the App Store from anywhere on the page.
2. Show the games themselves; the studio is the frame, not the subject.
3. Only say what is true today: a game in review or in development is labelled as such.
4. Fast on a phone over mobile data.
