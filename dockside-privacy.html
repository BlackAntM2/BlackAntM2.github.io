Privacy Policy — Dockside: Cast Off

Last updated: October 28, 2026

What Dockside stores

Dockside stores puzzle progress, best move counts, settings and local game state on your device. We do not operate a custom account system or receive this information.

Advertising

Dockside uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded hints and interstitial advertising. Google may process device information, advertising data and approximate location derived from an IP address to deliver, measure and protect advertising. Ads remain optional where the game offers a reward.

Tracking permission

Dockside may ask for App Tracking Transparency permission. If you allow it, Google may use the device advertising identifier to provide and measure more relevant advertising across companies' apps and websites. If you decline, the game remains fully playable and you may still see contextual ads.

Consent in the EU and UK

Where the law requires it, Dockside shows Google's consent message before any ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.

Game Center

If you choose to use Apple Game Center, Apple handles your Game Center account and nickname. Dockside submits puzzle scores to its leaderboard when Game Center is available. We do not receive your Apple ID or password.

What Dockside does not do

Dockside does not request your contacts, photos, microphone or precise location. We do not sell personal information, run a custom analytics server or require an account, subscription or purchase to play.

Your choices

You can change tracking permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. You can manage Game Center in your device settings. Removing the app removes locally stored game progress.
