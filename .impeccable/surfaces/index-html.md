## Scope

## Direction contract
THESIS: The studio is a shelf of cartridges you can pull. It refuses the category default of an icon grid of app cards with a hero above it.
OWN-WORLD: A painted pegboard wall in sunflower yellow with a cut-plywood ledge. Each game is an upright plastic cartridge in its own body colour (Dockside sea teal, Tap Against Time deep violet, Circuit Weave graphite), with grip ridges, a printed label carrying the real icon and art, and a price-tag Get button hanging under it. Dark ink type, chunky grotesk display.
STORY: Visitors see three real games at once, learn they are free iPhone games by one person, pull one to see it play, and tap Get.
FIRST VIEWPORT: On a 390pt phone, the wordmark and one line ("Small games. Finished. Free."), then the shelf with all three cartridges side by side at full width, each with its Get tag on the ledge. Pulling one lifts it out of the shelf.
