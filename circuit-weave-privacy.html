Privacy Policy — Circuit Weave

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Developer: ANTTECH SOLUTIONS
Contact: anttech1081@gmail.com

The short version

Circuit Weave collects no personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet.

What stays on your device

The game saves which circuits you have unlocked, your best move counts, whether you have seen the tutorial, and whether sound is on. This is stored only on your device. We never receive it. Deleting the app deletes it.

What Circuit Weave does not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services.
  • No account, sign-in or in-app purchase.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.
  • No data sold or shared with anyone.

Children

Circuit Weave does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that changes this, such as ads, this page will be updated before that version is released, with a new date above.
