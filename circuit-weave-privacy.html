Privacy Policy — Circuit Weave

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Developer: ANTTECH SOLUTIONS
Contact: anttech1081@gmail.com

The short version

Circuit Weave has no accounts and collects nothing about you itself. It shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google's ad software collects some device and advertising data to show and measure those ads. In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple.

What stays on your device

The game saves which circuits you have unlocked, your best move counts, how many hints you have, whether you bought Remove Ads, whether you have seen the tutorial, and whether sound is on. This is stored only on your device. We never receive it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Circuit Weave shows an optional video you can watch for a free hint, and a full-screen ad between some circuits (never in your first five, and never after you buy Remove Ads). These ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure them, Google may collect:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. If you decline, ads are not personalised.
  • Your approximate location, derived from your IP address.
  • Advertising data such as which ads were shown and tapped, and basic device and diagnostic information.

Players in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before any ad is requested, and can change that choice at any time from Shop › Privacy choices. You can also turn tracking off in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Google's use of this data is covered by Google's own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In-app purchases

Hint packs and Remove Ads are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, payment details or Apple Account.

What Circuit Weave does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services of our own.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • We do not sell your data.

Children

Circuit Weave is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this changes, this page will be updated before the version that changes it is released, with a new date above.
