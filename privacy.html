Privacy Policy for Tap Against Time

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Developer: ANTTECH SOLUTIONS (Durham, NC, USA)
Contact: anttech1081@gmail.com

The short version

  • There are no accounts. We don't ask for your name, email or any other personal information.
  • Your scores, progress and settings stay on your device.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device and ad data to show and measure those ads.
  • Personalized ads only happen if you allow tracking when iOS asks. You can change this at any time.
  • Purchases and Game Center leaderboards are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

1. Information stored on your device

To make the game work, Tap Against Time saves the following only on your device:

  • Best score, best stage and gameplay statistics
  • Achievements, daily challenge progress and streak
  • In-game currency (Volts) and unlocked ring skins
  • Settings such as sound, haptics, difficulty and language
  • Whether you have purchased “Remove Ads”

We do not operate servers and this data is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the game shows banner, interstitial (full-screen) and optional rewarded video ads provided by Google AdMob. To deliver, measure and limit fraud on these ads, Google may collect:

  • Device information, such as model, operating system, language and approximate location derived from IP address
  • Information about the ads you see and tap
  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking (see section 3)

Rewarded ads are always optional. You choose to watch one in exchange for a second chance or bonus Volts.

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

3. Tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency)

When you first open the game, iOS asks whether Tap Against Time may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. This is used only to show more relevant ads.

  • If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier is not shared and you will still see ads, just less personalized ones.
  • You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

4. In-app purchases (Apple)

“Remove Ads” is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we only receive confirmation that the purchase is valid. We never receive your name, Apple ID or payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Game Center (Apple)

If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores are submitted to Apple's Game Center leaderboards and shown with your Game Center nickname. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. You can sign out of Game Center in your device Settings.

6. What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't collect your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • The app contains no analytics or tracking tools other than those described above. If that changes, we will update this page first.

7. Children

Tap Against Time is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Tracking: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking
  • Personalized ads from Apple: Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising
  • Google ad settings: myadcenter.google.com
  • Your game data: view it in the app's Statistics screen. Delete it by deleting the app.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have rights to access or delete personal data. We don't hold personal data about you. For ad data, use Google's tools above, or contact us and we'll help.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above.
