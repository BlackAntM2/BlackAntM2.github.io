Privacy Policy — Raccoon Raid: Campsite Chaos

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Developer: ANTTECH SOLUTIONS
Contact: anttech1081@gmail.com

The short version

Raccoon Raid has no accounts and does not send anything about you to us. It shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google's ad software collects some device and advertising data to show and measure those ads. Leaderboards use Apple's Game Center. There are no in-app purchases.

What stays on your device

The game saves your stars, best scores, loot, cash, rank, raccoon crew, den upgrades, album, missions, daily streak, settings and whether you have seen the tutorial. It also keeps a small play log on your device (things like which campsite you played and when) so the game can show your own stats. All of this is stored only on your device. We never receive it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Raccoon Raid shows optional videos you can choose to watch for a reward (an extra raccoon, or a doubled loot crate), and a full-screen ad between some raids. These ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure them, Google may collect:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. If you decline, ads are not personalised.
  • Your approximate location, derived from your IP address.
  • Advertising data such as which ads were shown and tapped, and basic device and diagnostic information.

Players in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before any ad is requested, and can change that choice at any time from Credits › Privacy choices. You can also turn tracking off at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Google's use of this data is covered by Google's own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores are sent to Apple's Game Center leaderboards (daily, weekly, total stars and best haul) under your Game Center name. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. If you are not signed in, nothing is sent.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device (for example when your den is full or a new daily raid is ready). They are created on your iPhone; no notification server is involved and we receive nothing.

Sharing

When you tap Wanted Poster, the game makes a picture of your raid and opens the iOS share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you pick where to send it.

More games

The More Games screen asks the App Store which ANTTECH games are available, so it can show Get or Soon. That request contains only the games' App Store IDs, nothing about you.

What Raccoon Raid does not do

  • No account or sign-in of our own.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services of our own; the play log never leaves your device.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • We do not sell your data.

Children

Raccoon Raid is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this changes, this page will be updated before the version that changes it is released, with a new date above.
